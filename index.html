How it works
01
PDF parsing
PyPDF2 and pdfminer extract raw text from your resume PDF reliably across layouts.
02
Skill extraction
spaCy NLP + keyword matching identifies technical skills, tools, and soft skills.
03
JD analysis
Job description is parsed and skills are classified as required or preferred.
04
Gap scoring
Semantic similarity + exact matching produces a match score and ranked gap list.
Developer's Note: This tool uses NLP-based skill extraction to suggest gap areas. Results are indicative — always verify against the actual job posting and consult career advisors for important applications.
